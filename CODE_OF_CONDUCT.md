# HosurOne Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of **HosurOne** are committed to making participation in our project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or technical background.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and trustworthy regional community portal.

---

## 1. Respectful Collaboration

- Treat all community members, volunteer reviewers, and contributors with courtesy and empathy.
- Recognize that contributors come from diverse linguistic, cultural, and technical backgrounds.
- Provide constructive, friendly, and actionable feedback during review.
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism and suggestions for improvement.

---

## 2. Integrity & Zero Fabrication

- **Truth in Data:** All contributions must represent accurate, verifiable physical reality. Never submit invented, guessed, or satirical information.
- **No Deceptive Listings:** Submitting fake government contacts, nonexistent emergency numbers, or phantom bank branches endangers real citizens and will result in immediate disqualification and removal.
- **Acknowledge Uncertainty:** If you are unsure of a detail (e.g. exact closing time or pincode), omit the field or flag it for volunteer corroboration.

---

## 3. No Spam or Promotional Abuse

- HosurOne is an objective, non-commercial regional civic guide.
- Do not use listing submissions for keyword stuffing, search engine optimization (SEO) abuse, affiliate link placement, or spam advertising.
- Commercial listings (restaurants, shops, bakeries) are welcomed when they genuinely serve local residents, but promotional hype, misleading claims ("World's Best Doctor"), or aggressive brand campaigning are prohibited.

---

## 4. Respect for Privacy & Sensitive Information

- **Public Information Only:** Only submit telephone numbers, emails, and addresses that are published on public signboards, commercial literature, or official directories.
- **Personal Mobile Numbers:** Do not publish unlisted personal mobile numbers, home addresses, or private details of individuals without their explicit written consent.
- **Photographic Privacy:** When contributing photographs of places, avoid capturing identifiable close-up faces of bystanders, children, or vehicle license plates without permission.

---

## 5. Non-Discrimination & Safety

We will not tolerate:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or derogatory comments related to race, religion, caste, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or language.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit permission.

---

## 6. Enforcement & Review Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, pull requests, issues, and directory records that do not adhere to this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a confidential discussion with the project maintainers or emailing the civic admin team.

---

*HosurOne Community Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, with region-specific data integrity provisions for civic information.*
